# DoctorCar privacy policy

*Last updated: 23 November 2026* · Italian version: [crslabs.github.io/privacy/doctorcar.html](https://crslabs.github.io/privacy/doctorcar.html)

DoctorCar is built to **collect no data**. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking services and no servers of its own.

## What data it handles and where it stays
- Everything you enter (vehicles, jobs, due dates, fill-ups, expenses, spare parts, notes) and invoice photos stay **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.
- With **sync** (optional) data travels **only between your paired devices**, on the same Wi‑Fi, encrypted (TLS 1.3). It doesn't go through any server.
- **Backups** are encrypted with a password you choose and go only where you decide (a file or a folder).

## When the app uses the internet
- **Update check** (can be turned off in Settings → Updates): once a week the app reads from GitHub which is the latest published version. None of your data is sent; GitHub, like any website, sees the phone's internet address. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- **Parking location** (only when you ask for it): the phone reads the position; the approximate address is worked out by the Android service.
- **Report a problem or an idea**: the app prepares an email; you send it yourself, with your email app, and only then does it reach crslabs.info@gmail.com. You can see the whole text first, including the technical information (version, phone, latest errors: no number plates, names, amounts or photos), which can be removed.
- **Support DoctorCar**: opens the Ko-fi page in the browser only if you tap it. Any donations are handled by Ko-fi with PayPal or Stripe, under their own policies.

## Android permissions
- **Camera**: for invoice photos and to scan the pairing QR code.
- **Location**: only for «I parked here», when you tap it.
- **Notifications**: for due-date and parking reminders.
- **Fingerprint / PIN**: to open the app and confirm sensitive operations, if you turn it on.
- **Install apps**: to install updates (Android always asks for confirmation).

## Deleting your data
Settings → Data → «Delete everything from this device», or uninstall the app.
